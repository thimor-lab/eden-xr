# EdenXR — Hand of the Gods

A full-immersion **Android XR** WebXR god-game prototype inspired by Lionhead Studios' *Black & White* (2001) and *Black & White 2* (2005).

## Play on Android XR
1. Open **Google Chrome** on your Android XR headset (e.g., Samsung Galaxy XR).
2. Navigate to `https://thimor-lab.github.io/eden-xr/`.
3. Tap **Enter VR (Android XR)** and grant hand-tracking permissions if prompted.

## Core Mechanics
- **25-Joint God Hands**: Both hands render as alignment-reactive God Hands (golden ivory for Good, volcanic obsidian for Evil).
- **World-Pull Locomotion & Two-Hand Scale**: Pull the island with one hand, pinch-spread with two hands to zoom from cloud-level deity down to villager eye-level.
- **Physics Grab & Throw**: Pinch boulders, uproot trees, lift villagers, or raise/lower the Village Totem.
- **3D Air-Drawn Miracles**: Trace symbols in mid-air (`O` Food, `Δ` Wood, `~` Rain, `@` Fireball, `Z` Lightning) fueled by Prayer Power from worshipping villagers.
- **Influence Ring & Two Paths to Victory**: Grow your village and Impressiveness to peacefully convert the rival village, or crush them with boulders and destructive miracles.
- **Learning Creature (BDI + Operant Conditioning)**: Watch your giant Guardian Creature imitate you, and **pet** (praise) or **slap** (punish) it directly with your hands to shape its morality and habits.
